# Claim Appeals Process
Synthetic policy text for demonstration only. Not real payer rules.

A first-level appeal must be filed within 120 days of the date on the remittance advice that shows the denial. Appeals filed after 120 days are rejected unless good cause is documented.

A first-level appeal must include the denial letter or remittance, the original claim, and supporting clinical documentation. Appeals for denial code CO-50 must also include a letter of medical necessity signed by the treating provider.

Northwind Health decides first-level appeals within 30 calendar days of receipt. For urgent pre-service appeals the decision is made within 72 hours.

A second-level appeal may be filed within 60 days of the first-level decision. A second-level appeal is reviewed by a physician who was not involved in the first decision. After second-level appeal, the provider may request an independent external review.
