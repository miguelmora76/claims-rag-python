# Coordination of Benefits
Synthetic policy text for demonstration only. Not real payer rules.

When a member has more than one health plan, the primary plan pays first. For a dependent child covered by both parents, the birthday rule applies: the plan of the parent whose birthday falls earlier in the calendar year is primary.

Medicare is secondary to an employer group plan when the employer has 20 or more employees and the member is covered through current employment. Otherwise Medicare is primary.

Secondary claims must include the primary plan's remittance advice showing the amount paid and the adjustment reasons. A secondary claim submitted without the primary remittance is denied with code CO-22.

Northwind Health pays as secondary only up to the amount it would have paid as primary, minus what the primary plan already paid.
