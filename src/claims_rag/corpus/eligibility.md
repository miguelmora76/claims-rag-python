# Eligibility Verification
Synthetic policy text for demonstration only. Not real payer rules.

Providers should verify member eligibility on the date of service using the real-time eligibility transaction (270/271) before rendering non-emergency services. A 271 response is valid only for the date it was requested.

Northwind Health does not guarantee payment based on a verification response. Coverage can be terminated retroactively for non-payment of premium, with a grace period of 30 days.

If a claim is denied with code CO-27, the member's coverage had terminated before the date of service. The provider should bill the patient or any other coverage, after checking for a newer plan on file.

Verification responses include the member's copay, deductible remaining, and out-of-pocket maximum remaining. Deductible remaining is the figure used to estimate patient responsibility (PR-1).
