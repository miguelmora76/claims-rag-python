# Prior Authorization Requirements
Synthetic policy text for demonstration only. Not real payer rules.

Prior authorization is required for advanced imaging (MRI, CT, PET), elective inpatient admissions, and specialty infusion drugs. Primary care visits and routine labs do not require prior authorization.

Standard prior authorization requests receive a decision within 5 business days. Urgent requests receive a decision within 24 hours when the provider attests that delay could seriously jeopardize the member's health.

An approved authorization is valid for 60 days from the approval date. If the service is not performed in that window, the provider must request a new authorization.

A claim for a service that required prior authorization but had none is denied with code CO-197. The provider may request retroactive authorization within 14 days of the date of service, only for emergency admissions.
