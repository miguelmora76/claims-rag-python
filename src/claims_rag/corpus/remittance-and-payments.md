# Remittance and Payment Timing
Synthetic policy text for demonstration only. Not real payer rules.

Clean claims submitted electronically are paid within 14 calendar days of receipt. Clean paper claims are paid within 30 calendar days. A clean claim has no missing data and needs no additional review.

Payments are issued by electronic funds transfer, and the electronic remittance advice (ERA, format 835) is delivered the same day as the payment. Providers must enroll in EFT before the first payment is issued.

If a claim is not paid within the required period, interest of 1.5 percent per month accrues on the unpaid clean claim amount, starting on day 15 for electronic claims.

Overpayment recoveries are offset against future payments after a 30-day notice. A provider can dispute an overpayment recovery in writing within those 30 days.
