# Timely Filing Policy
Synthetic policy text for demonstration only. Not real payer rules.

Northwind Health requires in-network providers to submit initial claims within 90 days of the date of service. Out-of-network providers have 180 days from the date of service.

Corrected claims must be submitted within 365 days of the date of service. A corrected claim must reference the original claim number in the frequency code field, or it will be treated as a new claim and may be denied as a duplicate.

If the claim was delayed because the member's coverage was retroactively added, the filing period starts on the date Northwind Health notified the provider of eligibility. Attach the eligibility notification as proof.

Proof of timely filing is a clearinghouse acceptance report showing the original submission date. A paper claim receipt without a date stamp is not accepted as proof.
